Pylon is the first agentic support platform built for B2B.
Track customer issues across any channel (Slack Connect, Email, Microsoft Teams, Chat Widget, Forms, ...) and orchestrate agents to investigate and act on your customer issues.
What is Agentic Support?
It's a new way of working, where instead of doing the work yourself - finding the right article in the docs, opening a new tab to write a SQL query to check logs, finding an old slack thread with a workaround - you direct agents to do the work for you.
This means support teams become agent builders and orchestrators - building agents and nudging them along on tickets while the agents learn how it is that you solve ever ticket and get better and faster each interaction. It's your judgement and direction, with the tedious work handed off to agents.

Pylon vs Salesforce Service Cloud for B2B Support
Pylon vs. Salesforce
Salesforce wasn’t built for AI. Pylon was.
Salesforce Service Cloud gives enterprises a powerful platform for CRM, service, automation, and customer engagement. But Agentforce is only as useful as the data, workflows, and actions your Salesforce team configures around it.
Pylon gives B2B support teams agents that investigate, coordinate, and act with the customer’s account, product, and support context already loaded. Your team directs the work instead of waiting on another Salesforce project.

Start with the investigation already done
Agentforce Service Agent can process cases, answer questions, and take configured actions. Pylon’s Background Agents begin the internal investigation as soon as the right support signal occurs.
They gather account context, investigate connected systems, find related issues, and run the right Skills before a human opens the ticket. Your team starts with evidence and recommended next steps instead of another case to read and research.
Give agents context beyond the CRM
Salesforce has deep CRM context. Companies with mature Salesforce data models can give Agentforce access to accounts, contracts, entitlements, case history, knowledge, and other connected records.
Pylon combines Salesforce data with conversations, Slack history, product usage, logs, engineering systems, and completed investigations. That raw data builds standing Account, Support, and Product Intelligence that agents use to investigate and act, not just generate a response.
Turn support processes into Skills your team can own
Salesforce supports natural-language instructions alongside subagents, actions, Flow, Apex, permissions, and other platform controls. That flexibility is powerful, but production changes can depend on Salesforce admins, RevOps, IT, or developers.
Pylon Skills combine instructions, context, tools, permissions, and approval steps in plain language. The same Skill can be used by Assist Agent, Background Agents, Support Agent, or a person. The support team can run, review, and improve the process directly.

Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between Pylon and Salesforce Service Cloud?
Salesforce Service Cloud is an enterprise CRM extension that is powerful for teams already deep in the Salesforce ecosystem, but complex to configure, slow to implement, and not built around the channels B2B customers use today like Slack, Teams, and Discord. Pylon is a purpose-built agentic support platform that is live in weeks, not months.
‍
Pylon is an agentic support platform built for complex B2B work. Its agents use Support, Account, and Product Intelligence to gather context, investigate issues, coordinate across systems, and act while humans direct and review the work.
Is Pylon a good fit for teams that use Salesforce?
Yes. Pylon can use Salesforce as the CRM and system of record while giving the support team a separate system for conversations, investigations, account context, and agent workflows.
‍
Relevant Salesforce data becomes part of Account Intelligence alongside context from Slack, email, product usage, logs, engineering systems, and prior support work.
How does Pylon’s AI compare with Agentforce?
Both platforms offer customer-facing resolution, interactive assistance, natural-language configuration, testing, and connections to external tools.
‍
The difference is the operating model and context behind the agents. Agentforce uses Salesforce data, knowledge, workflows, and configured actions. Pylon’s agents use standing B2B account, product, and support intelligence. Background Agents also begin investigating before a person opens the issue.
Can Agentforce use data outside Salesforce?
Yes. Salesforce can connect Agentforce to external data and tools through Data 360, integrations, APIs, MuleSoft, and MCP.

Salesforce Integration | Pylon
Salesforce
Sync customer context between Salesforce and Pylon automatically.
Trusted by 1,500+ B2B customers globally
Why teams use it
Deploy AI agents that handle questions end-to-end, gather context for your team, and escalate to humans when it matters.
Keep accounts and contacts in sync automatically
Pylon matches Salesforce and Pylon accounts by domain and name (or custom fields), and automatically adds new contacts from customer channels to the right Salesforce Account. Synced fields flow both ways, so they're usable in Pylon views, triggers, and macros.
See the full customer story without switching tabs
Every day, Pylon summarizes customer conversations with AI and syncs them to the relevant Salesforce Account. Then it surfaces details right in the Issue sidebar while you work.
Launch customer channels straight from Salesforce
Click "Create Slack Channel" from Salesforce to spin up a named channel, auto-invite all Opportunity Contact Roles, enable Pylon, and send your configured welcome message to invitees — all in one action.
Target work and outreach using Salesforce data
Filter and view Issues by any Salesforce attribute, and filter Broadcasts to reach customers based on data stored in Salesforce.
How to install
Step 1
Open the Salesforce integration

Frequently asked questions
How do I connect Salesforce to Pylon?
Navigate to the App Directory from your Pylon dashboard and click on the Salesforce integration to follow the setup instructions. Accounts are mapped using domain and name matching between Salesforce and Pylon by default,  though the integration can also be configured to match on custom Pylon account fields.
What data syncs between Salesforce and Pylon?
The Salesforce integration is bidirectional. Pylon scans your customer channels daily and uses AI to summarize conversations, syncing those summaries to the relevant Account in Salesforce. You can also sync fields either from Pylon to Salesforce or from Salesforce to Pylon. Synced fields are then available across all Pylon features like filters in views, triggers, and macros. New contacts who join a channel are also automatically added to Salesforce and linked to the relevant Account.
Can I create Slack channels for customers directly from Salesforce?
Yes. Pylon's Salesforce widget lets you click "Create Slack Channel" from within a Salesforce record, name the channel, and Pylon will automatically handle the rest, including channel setup and customer onboarding. You can also send Broadcasts to customer segments based on Salesforce data or filter Pylon issues by any Salesforce attribute
How often does Pylon sync data with Salesforce?
Pylon matches accounts using domain and name by default, with account mappings viewable and updatable in the Account Mapping tab. Syncing occurs several times a day, pulling defined attributes from Salesforce into the respective Pylon objects.
Can I send customer Broadcasts based on Salesforce data?
Yes. You can send a Broadcast to a set of customers based on data stored in Salesforce, and you can view and filter Pylon Issues by any attributes from Salesforce.  Salesforce fields synced into Pylon are available as filters across Views, Triggers, and Macros throughout the platform.
