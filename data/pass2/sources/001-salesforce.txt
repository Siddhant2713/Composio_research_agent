What is Salesforce?
Salesforce is the
#1 AI CRM
(customer relationship management) platform. We bring companies and customers together by providing a unified set of applications — powered by
agentic AI
and data — that help every department, including sales, service, marketing, commerce, and IT, work as one. You can learn more about
what Salesforce is
and
what Salesforce does
by visiting
our official resources
. Our cloud-based agentic solutions help you find more leads, close more deals, and provide better service to your customers, all on a single, integrated agentic platform.
What is CRM software and why do businesses in India need it?
CRM
stands for
customer relationship management.
It’s a technology for managing all your company’s relationships and interactions with customers and potential customers. A CRM system helps you:
• Organise
contact information, leads, and customer service data
• Automate
tasks like logging calls and sending follow-up emails
• Analyse
data to understand customer behaviour and business performance
A CRM, especially one powered by AI like Salesforce, is essential for growth because it gives you a
single
,
360-degree view
of every customer
, allowing you to build stronger, more profitable relationships at scale.
What is agentic AI and how is it used in Salesforce?
Agentic AI
refers to advanced artificial intelligence systems that can act
autonomously
to achieve a specific goal with minimal human intervention. Unlike traditional
AI
that only generates content or analyses data, agentic AI can reason, plan multistep actions, and execute tasks.
In Salesforce, agentic AI — part of our
Agentforce
technology — is used to:
• Automate
complex workflows like lead qualification or service case resolution
• Predict
business outcomes, such as which deals are likely to close
• Personalise
every customer interaction across marketing, sales, and service channels
How does Salesforce CRM help businesses grow in India?
Salesforce offers agentic CRM solutions tailored for companies of all sizes, from
startups and small businesses
to large global enterprises. Our Agentforce 360 Platform is flexible and scalable, allowing you to start with the essentials you need — like sales, service, commerce, or marketing — and easily add more functionality as your business grows. This ensures you only pay for what you use and have an
AI and CRM
platform that can grow with your business.
Salesforce Starter
helps you hit the ground running for free, providing an all-in-one suite of sales, service, and marketing tools designed specifically for small business needs. The setup process is simple so you can focus on building customer relationships immediately, with the peace of mind that the full power of the Salesforce ecosystem is ready whenever you are.

What is the AgentExchange ecosystem?
The
AgentExchange Ecosystem
is Salesforce's broader partner ecosystem for bringing additional headless capabilities to customers.
Powered by the
Headless Toolkit
, it enables technology, solution, data, model, and consulting partners to connect Salesforce with partner-built agents, applications, and interfaces. Through AgentExchange, customers can discover, deploy, and scale these capabilities without adding fragmented tools or operating models.
What is AgentExchange?
AgentExchange is the unified marketplace to discover, buy, and deploy trusted agents, apps, and capabilities that extend Salesforce wherever your people and agents work.
AI-driven discovery and embedded experiences help you find and activate relevant solutions directly in Agentforce or Slack. Private offers, unified billing, and automated provisioning simplify procurement. You can also extend Salesforce to authorised AI tools such as Claude, Codex, and Cursor with reusable skills and plugins, or connect third-party MCP servers to Agentforce and Slack.
Are AppExchange apps still available?
All the AppExchange solutions you know and trust remain a core part of your experience. You can still access the full ecosystem of over 9,000 vetted apps to secure and strengthen your Salesforce foundation. Everything is conveniently organised under the Apps category on the
AgentExchange homepage.
What Are Salesforce Skills and Plugins?
Salesforce skills
are reusable capabilities designed to work across supported AI agents, including Agentforce, Claude, and Codex. Think of a skill like a USB-C cable that connects to multiple platforms.
Plugins are platform-specific packages that can bundle multiple skills and connections. Plugins on AgentExchange currently support Claude, with support for additional AI agents planned.
Learn more in this blog.
What is a Salesforce partner?
Salesforce partners
are trusted experts who help businesses extend and maximise the platform's value through two primary categories available on AgentExchange. Independent Software Vendors (ISVs) build the third-party apps, agents, and integrations that add specialised functionality to your environment, while consulting and implementation partners provide the strategic guidance and technical services needed to integrate Salesforce products into your specific business workflows. Whether you need a pre-built solution or a custom digital transformation, our partners provide the expertise to help you succeed.
How is the Salesforce Customer Success Group different from a Salesforce partner?
The Salesforce Customer Success Group is a team of Salesforce employees who are experts at working with our partners to help customers launch their implementations and maximise the value of their Salesforce solutions.
How can a Salesforce partner help me?
Salesforce partners provide the specialised expertise needed to transform the platform into a tailored business engine built for your specific goals. Fundamentally, they integrate Salesforce products into your existing technical ecosystem and customise features—such as tailoring Agentforce for Sales for unique lead-management workflows—to align with your specific business processes.
