Team Users: A Quick Overview
Organization vs. Team levels in
Zoho CRM
Data in
Zoho CRM
is organized into data modules, and your CRM users are granted access to the modules relevant to them. Modules and users are available at two levels — the
organization (or org) level
, and
team level
.
Org modules
contain data closely tied to your sales processes. The users who work on these modules are
org users
, and they have full-scale CRM access. This license is ideal for sales reps, sales managers, presales teams, and central CRM admins.
Note:
Any mention of just "users" in
Zoho CRM
refers to this type of user.
Team modules
contain niche data particular to specific teams that support the sales process at various stages. The users who work on these modules are
team users
. Outside of their team modules, they only have partial access to CRM data and functionality. This license is ideal for all non-sales users, like marketing, support, legal, finance, and others.
The following table provides a short overview of what team users can (and cannot) do in both types of modules
