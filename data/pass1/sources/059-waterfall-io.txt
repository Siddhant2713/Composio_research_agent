===== https://www.waterfall.io/pricing
TITLE: Pricing | How Waterfall.io pricing works
ACCESSED: 2026-09-27

The model
Three rules that decide what you pay
A miss costs nothing
Billing
We are charged by our vendors on every request we make on your behalf, including the ones that come back empty. You are not. A credit is deducted only when we hand back a verified result, so the risk of a low-coverage file sits with us rather than with your budget.
One credit covers the whole waterfall
A single request may pass through several of our 30+ sources before one returns a verified answer. You are charged once, for the answer. This is the structural difference from buying vendors directly, where each contract you add multiplies your cost per record whether or not that vendor was the one that found it.
Rates are quoted against your data
Commercials
Per-credit rates depend on the endpoints you use and the volume you commit to. We quote after running a benchmark on a sample of your own records, because a list price set against someone else's file tells you nothing about what your file will cost.
Why there is no public rate card
Straight answer
Because a per-record price is not comparable across vendors and we would rather not pretend otherwise. Two providers quoting the same cent-per-email can differ by a factor of three on what fraction of your file they actually resolve, and the cheaper one is usually the more expensive one once you divide by results. The number that matters is your cost per verified result on your records, and the only honest way to produce it is to run both against the same file. We will do that before quoting, and we will show you the working.
What moves your cost per result
The identifier you send
A professional email or a LinkedIn URL resolves more often, and in fewer hops, than a name plus a company domain. Better inputs mean fewer paid-for misses upstream and a lower blended cost.
Which endpoints you call
Mobile numbers cost more than emails because the underlying data costs more. Job-change checks are cheap to run at scale because unchanged contacts are never billed.
How hard your records are
Enterprise contacts in North America resolve on the first source. Smaller companies, non-standard email patterns and coverage outside a vendor's home region go deeper into the waterfall — the same price to you, a different cost to us.
What you commit to
Per-credit rates fall with committed volume and term. Platforms reselling enrichment downstream are priced differently from teams enriching their own CRM, because the volume shape and the support load are different.

===== https://www.waterfall.io/product/contact-search
TITLE: Contact search | Search any contact, at any company
ACCESSED: 2026-09-27

The core job
How to turn a personal email address into a business profile
Send the address you already have to a resolution API rather than matching it against a static database. Contact Search accepts a personal email — Gmail, Outlook, or any consumer domain — and returns the person behind it: current company, job title, seniority, LinkedIn profile and a verified work email. That single step is what turns an anonymous self-serve signup into a lead you can route, score and assign.
Start from whatever identifier you actually have
Most search tools require the one identifier they happen to index, which is rarely the one sitting in your record. Contact Search resolves from a personal email, a professional email, a social profile URL, or a name plus domain — whichever your row contains. The hardest and most common case is the personal address left behind by a self-serve signup, and it is the case this endpoint was built for.
Resolved against live sources, then verified
Accuracy
A match is only useful if the person is still where the record says they are. Search runs against live sources rather than a periodic snapshot, and any email returned is verified before delivery — so a resolved profile is one you can route, score and write back to the CRM without a second check.
Pay Only for Matching Profiles
Pricing
We offer outcome-based pricing — Waterfall deducts credits only when we return a matching profile, even though we incur costs querying our vendors on every request. Searches that come back empty are free, and one credit covers the whole waterfall however many sources it takes to resolve the identifier you sent.
Four jobs that start with an identifier you cannot use yet
Signup routing and lead scoring
Self-serve signups arrive as personal Gmail and Outlook addresses with no company attached. Resolve them to a company, title and seniority and routing, scoring and territory assignment become possible at all — rather than everyone landing in the same unqualified bucket.
Reverse lookup on unknown senders
A form fill or a reply arrives from an address nobody recognises. Resolve it to a person, a title and a company before a rep spends time qualifying it by hand.
Gap-filling on partial records
Rows that arrived from an event list, a legacy import or an acquisition often carry one usable field and nothing else. Search resolves from whichever identifier survived, rather than requiring the one you no longer have.
Identity resolution for AI agents
An agent cannot reason about an account until it knows who it is looking at. One call, one schema, whatever identifier the workflow happens to hold — and a deterministic answer rather than a guess the model has to defend.
